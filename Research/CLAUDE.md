# CLAUDE.md — Research

Scientific and strategic background research. Root `CLAUDE.md` rules apply. This
file adds research-specific context.

## Folder layout

- `industry/` — sector trends, publications, regulatory landscape
- `technical/` — scientific and technical references
- `notes/` — working notes, meeting summaries, synthesis (default save location)
- `sources/` — raw source documents and references

## Standing rules

- Distinguish preclinical vs clinical evidence explicitly on every claim.
- Distinguish imaging relevance vs therapeutic relevance explicitly. Critical
  for RLT target selection.
- Cite primary sources (author, journal/year, or guidance date). Flag gaps
  rather than fabricating. If a number or citation is not known, say so.
- Tie findings back to target selection, translational feasibility, and clinical
  positioning.
