## Contract review standard

When reviewing or redlining any partner agreement, flag by severity
(green/yellow/red) and name the specific clause. High-severity categories to
always surface: feedback / IP assignment, usage-data aggregation, liability
caps, model-training language, term lock-in, termination rights. Precedent
pattern from prior reviews (TandemAI, BenchSci): irrevocable feedback
assignment, usage-data aggregation, and low liability caps are the recurring
red flags. Do not upload proprietary data before redlines are resolved. Greg
owns contract review; recommend legal sign-off before execution.
