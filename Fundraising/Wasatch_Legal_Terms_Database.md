### 1B. Additional Purchasers (SPA §1.3(a))

After the Initial Closing, the Company may sell authorized but unsold Seed Preferred shares to Additional Purchasers, subject to:

- Lead Investor (Wasatch ST QOZ Fund) reasonable approval required
- Must close within 365 days of Initial Closing (or as agreed with Lead Investor)
- Each Additional Purchaser must execute the IRA, Voting Agreement, and ROFR/Co-Sale Agreement
- Total authorized: 800,000,000 shares of Seed Preferred (i.e., up to $80M at $0.10/share)

### 1C. Second Funding Agreement / $20M Convertible Debt Commitment (SPA §6.16)

**Trigger:** If the Company sells all 800,000,000 authorized Seed Preferred shares ($80M raised), the Lead Investor commits to provide an additional $20M.

**Structure:** "Preferred convertible debt" (or other mutually agreed debt/equity structure).

**Key terms:**

- Term: ≥12 months
- Interest: 12% (paid monthly)
- Origination fee: 2%
- Other terms: typical for transaction of this nature, determined in good faith

**Gating condition:** All 800M shares must be sold. This is a conditional commitment, not automatic.

### 1D. Right of First Offer for Major Investors (IRA §4.1)

Major Investors have pro rata rights on any New Securities issuance, subject to:

- **Major Investor threshold:** Hold ≥50,000,000 Registrable Securities (adjusted for splits)
- **Notice period:** 14 days to elect after Offer Notice
- **Overallotment:** Fully Exercising Investors may purchase unsubscribed shares (10-day window)
- **Company closing window:** 120 days to sell unsubscribed shares to third parties at ≥Offer Notice price
- **Use-or-lose:** Right terminates for any Major Investor who fails to purchase full pro rata in any transaction

**Carve-outs (not subject to ROFO):**

- (i) Exempted Securities (as defined in Certificate of Incorporation)
- (ii) IPO shares
- (iii) Preferred Stock issued under the SPA
- (iv) Equity issued under the Second Funding Agreement

**Alternative compliance (IRA §4.1(f)):** Company may issue first, then notify Major Investors within 30 days; investors have 14 days to purchase up to their maintenance amount.

**Termination:** Pre-IPO or Deemed Liquidation Event.

### 1E. CFIUS / Foreign Person Cap (IRA §5.12)

No Foreign Person may obtain >10% voting equity interest without Board approval. No Foreign Person may obtain DPA Triggering Rights without Board approval.
