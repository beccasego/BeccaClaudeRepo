## Notes & Data Gaps

- **Aktis A vs. A Ext**: $72M Series A (MPM, EcoR1, Vida, 2021) + $84M extension (Aug 2022) = $156M total A-stage. Confirmed via PitchBook and Aktis press release.
- **AdvanCell Series A**: Amount not publicly disclosed. Series B was denominated in AUD (A$18M ≈ $12M USD). Total ~$132M per Crunchbase.
- **Radionetics "Series B"**: The $140M from Lilly is a strategic upfront payment tied to an exclusive acquisition option ($1B). Not a traditional venture round — treat with caution in pure VC comp analysis.
- **Ratio Seed**: Described as ">$20M" at launch; funded via development alliances with Bayer and Lantheus. Exact amount not precisely disclosed.
- **Point SPAC**: ~$300M total proceeds = ~$136M from SPAC trust + ~$165M PIPE (led by RA Capital).
- **Alpha-9**: Rebranded from Alpha-9 Theranostics to Alpha-9 Oncology between Series B and C.
