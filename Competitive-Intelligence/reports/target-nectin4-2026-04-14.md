# Nectin-4 — Target Landscape CI Report
**Date:** 2026-04-14
**Prepared by:** Claude Code
**Type:** Target Sweep

---

## TL;DR
Nectin-4 is a crowded but still-expanding oncology target anchored by enfortumab vedotin (EV/Padcev), which is actively gaining new indications in muscle-invasive bladder cancer. At least 7 next-gen ADCs are in clinical-stage development with differentiated payloads, modalities (bispecific, radiopharmaceutical), and indication strategies targeting EV's safety liabilities (neuropathy) and coverage gaps (post-EV patients). The field is expanding rapidly into cervical, TNBC, HNSCC, pancreatic, and vulvar cancers.

## EV / Padcev — Approved Agent Deep Dive

**Current approved indications:**
- Locally advanced/metastatic urothelial carcinoma (monotherapy + EV+pembro)
- Perioperative MIBC — cisplatin-ineligible (approved **November 2025**)
- Perioperative MIBC — cisplatin-eligible: **Priority Review pending** (EMA validated March 2026)

**Key clinical data:**
- KEYNOTE-905 (EV-303): EV+pembro showed superior EFS and OS vs radical cystectomy alone in cisplatin-eligible MIBC
- EV-PRIME trial (NCT06470282): now exploring EV+pembro+radiation as bladder-preserving strategy

**Commercial context:** Astellas + Pfizer co-commercializing in US; Merck (Keytruda partner). Revenue meaningful — MIBC expansion significantly broadens addressable market.

## Key Signals & Observations

**Modality diversification is accelerating:** The field is no longer just MMAE-ADC. Radiopharmaceuticals (Aktis), bispecific ADCs (Avenzo), and immunotherapy engineering (TIGIT-Nectin-4) are now entering or approaching the clinic. This suggests saturation of the first-gen ADC space and a search for differentiated mechanisms.

**Safety differentiation is the primary ADC competitive axis:** ADRX-0706's reported lower neuropathy profile is the most-cited differentiator vs EV. Peripheral neuropathy is EV's key liability — any next-gen program with cleaner safety will find a patient population in post-EV or combination settings.

**Post-EV patient population is emerging as a target market:** CRB-701 and others are explicitly positioning in the post-EV setting, which will grow as EV penetrates MIBC perioperative use.

**China-originated programs (Hengrui, MabWorks) are in platform trials:** These are not yet clearly headed for Western markets but represent pipeline intelligence on payload and clinical strategies.

**New indications: pancreatic and vulvar are newly characterized (2026):** Both are under-served with high unmet need. Early publications suggest Nectin-4 expression is meaningful in both — watch for IND filings.

**Radiopharmaceutical angle (Aktis) is well-capitalized:** $318M IPO + pharma backing from Lilly, Merck, Novartis, BMS is unusually strong. AKY-1189 in NECTINIUM-2 trial is a signal that large pharma sees Nectin-4 RLT as viable.

## White Space / Opportunities for Sego

- **Post-EV setting:** Growing unmet need as EV penetrates MIBC — combination or subsequent-line positioning
- **Pancreatic cancer:** Newly characterized Nectin-4 expression + high unmet need + immune-evasion mechanism described (2026 Trends in Cancer) = early-mover opportunity
- **Vulvar SCC:** Rare, high-Nectin-4, no approved targeted therapy — potential orphan angle
- **Combination with IO:** EV-PRIME is exploring radiation + EV + IO for bladder preservation; broader IO combinations in non-urothelial tumors are largely open
- **Theranostics / imaging:** Radiotracers for Nectin-4 PET imaging could enable patient selection across all indications — platform opportunity
