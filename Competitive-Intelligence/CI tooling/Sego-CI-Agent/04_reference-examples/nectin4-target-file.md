## TL;DR

The Nectin-4 field advanced materially since the prior report. Three major developments since early April: (1) **Corbus received FDA alignment on its registrational path for CRB-701 in 2L HNSCC and cervical cancer** (April 7, 2026), putting the first next-gen ADC on a clear approval trajectory; (2) **Aktis completed a $365M IPO** and initiated the NECTINIUM-2 Phase 1b trial, positioning radiopharmaceuticals as a credible next-generation modality with Q1 2027 data expected; (3) **9MW2821 (bulumtatug fuvedotin) published Phase I/II data in Annals of Oncology** showing 54% ORR in urothelial and 50% in TNBC, with multiple pivotal trials now enrolling. The competitive field has expanded from ~7 clinical programs to 9+, and the range of tumor types with validated expression continues to grow — now including testicular GCT, gestational trophoblastic disease, penile cancer, and esophageal SCC.

> **Flag for Sego:** Avenzo's Fast Track for AVZO-103 in post-EV urothelial cancer (Nov 2025) directly codifies the post-EV setting as an FDA-recognized unmet need. This is the clearest signal yet that the FDA sees post-EV as a distinct therapeutic opportunity.

## Company Deep Dives — Major Updates

### Corbus Pharmaceuticals — CRB-701 ⚠️ NEW SINCE PRIOR REPORT

**ESMO 2025 data (Oct 2025):**
- Unconfirmed ORR at 3.6 mg/kg dose: HNSCC **47.6%**, Cervical **37.5%**, Bladder **55.6%**
- **No grade 4 or 5 treatment-related adverse events**
- Markedly low peripheral neuropathy and skin toxicity vs EV — central differentiating claim
- Phase 1/2 NCT06265727; US + Europe

**FDA alignment on registration path — April 7, 2026:**
- Corbus announced broad FDA alignment on registrational study design for **2L HNSCC vs chemotherapy** and **2L cervical cancer vs chemotherapy or Tivdak**
- Design enables potential **accelerated approval** pathway
- HNSCC registrational study initiation: **mid-2026**
- Combination CRB-701 + Keytruda in 1L HNSCC: data Q4 2026

**Financials (Dec 31, 2025):**
- Cash/investments: **$163.3M**; cash runway into **2028**
- Raised $75M in Q4 2025 public offering
- R&D expense: $70M in 2025 (vs $32M in 2024) — accelerating clinical investment
- Net loss FY2025: $78.5M

**What changed vs prior report:** Prior report had "ESMO 2025 data" listed as a planned event. Now confirmed: strong ORR data, safety profile confirmed differentiated, FDA registration path agreed. CRB-701 is the most clinically advanced next-gen Nectin-4 ADC in Western markets.

### Aktis Oncology — AKY-1189 ⚠️ NEW SINCE PRIOR REPORT

**IPO: January 8, 2026**
- Raised **$365.4M gross** (prior report stated $318M based on pre-pricing estimate; final raise was higher)
- Trades on Nasdaq: **AKTS**
- Backers: Lilly, Merck, Novartis, BMS (strategic investors retained)

**FDA Fast Track: February 2026**
- AKY-1189 granted Fast Track for **locally advanced/metastatic urothelial cancer** post prior systemic therapy
- Reinforces post-EV UC as FDA priority setting

**NECTINIUM-2 trial — INITIATED May 2025:**
- Phase 1b, [²²⁵Ac]AKY-1189, miniprotein radioconjugate
- Indications: mUC (dose escalation), then dedicated cohorts in mUC, TNBC, lung, colorectal, cervical (~150 patients total)
- Up to 6 doses per patient
- **Initial data expected Q1 2027**

**Pipeline expansion:** AKY-2519 IND cleared by FDA (Mar 2026) — targets B7-H3; shows Aktis is expanding its miniprotein radioconjugate platform beyond Nectin-4.

**What changed vs prior report:** IPO now completed ($365.4M, not $318M). Trial now actively enrolling. FDA Fast Track granted. Data timeline set for Q1 2027. Aktis is now a public company with >$300M+ cash and is the best-capitalized emerging player in the field.

### 9MW2821 (Bulumtatug Fuvedotin) — Phase I/II Published ⚠️ NEW SINCE PRIOR REPORT

**Ann Oncol, Aug 2025 (PMID: 40288679):**
- 274 patients enrolled; Phase I/II FIH; NCT05216965
- RP2D: 1.25 mg/kg (days 1, 8, 15 of 28-day cycle)
- MTD not reached; 1 DLT (grade 4 neutropenia >5 days at 1.5 mg/kg)
- **ORR at 1.25 mg/kg:**
  - Urothelial: **54.1%** (n=51)
  - TNBC: **50%** (n=20)
  - Cervical: **32.1%** (n=62)
  - Esophageal: **14.0%** (n=49)
- **Multiple pivotal trials now in progress:** NCT06196736, NCT06592326, NCT06692166
- Structural basis of binding published: J Biol Chem, Dec 2025 (PMID: 41101504)

**What changed vs prior report:** Prior report listed 9MW2821 as a Phase 2 platform trial. Now Phase I/II data are published in a top journal with competitive ORR numbers and multiple pivotal trials initiated. This is a serious registrational program, likely initially in China but with global ambitions.

### Avenzo Therapeutics — AVZO-103 ⚠️ UPDATE SINCE PRIOR REPORT

**Phase 1/2 FIH INITIATED: September 2025**
- Open-label, first-in-human study of AVZO-103 (Nectin-4 × TROP2 bispecific ADC)
- Single agent and combination; advanced solid tumors

**FDA Fast Track: November 2025**
- Indication: locally advanced/metastatic **urothelial cancer previously treated with enfortumab vedotin** (post-EV setting)
- Rationale: Nectin-4 + TROP2 co-expression; dual antigen targeting to overcome EV resistance mechanisms; no approved ADC exists for post-EV UC patients

**What changed vs prior report:** Prior report had $60M Series B and Phase 1/2 initiation pending. Now trial is active, and FDA Fast Track specifically for post-EV urothelial cancer is a direct signal about unmet need in that setting.

## Competitive Implications for Sego

### Threats

- **Corbus CRB-701 is now the most direct threat in 2L HNSCC and cervical cancer:** FDA has agreed on a registration path. If mid-2026 data confirms durability, CRB-701 could be the first next-gen Nectin-4 ADC to file for approval in Western markets, potentially crowding HNSCC and cervical before a Sego program could enter.
- **9MW2821 pivotal trials** (urothelial, cervical, TNBC, esophageal) are running in parallel across multiple registrational cohorts — China-first but will expand West. High ORR data already published.
- **Avenzo's post-EV Fast Track** signals that FDA explicitly views post-EV UC as an addressable unmet need. Any program Sego considers for urothelial post-EV positioning is entering a validated but crowded space.
- **Aktis's $365M and pharma backing** (Lilly, Merck, Novartis, BMS) make the radiopharmaceutical modality a well-resourced threat; data expected Q1 2027.

### Opportunities / White Space

- **Combination strategies:** The HER2 + Nectin-4 ADC combo paper (Cancer Cell Int, Mar 2026) and Trop2 + Nectin-4 co-expression in esophageal SCC (Pathol Res Pract, Jul 2025) suggest combination approaches remain mechanistically interesting and largely un-validated clinically.
- **Newly characterized tumor types with no claimed programs:**
  - Testicular GCT (new expression data, no programs)
  - Gestational trophoblastic disease (expression confirmed, no programs)
  - Penile cancer (28% expression, noted as promising but trial-stage only)
  - NMIBC (BCG-unresponsive setting; ADCs being explored intravesically — novel delivery angle)
- **Theranostics / PET imaging:** The Sci Adv paper on Nectin-4 PET for EV dose-response prediction (Jan 2026) supports a companion diagnostics/patient selection platform. No company has filed on this; Aktis's miniprotein platform could enable it, but it's not their stated lead.
- **Sialylation + Nectin-4 combo:** New Cancer Res paper (Jan 2026) shows sialylation targeting enhances EV efficacy. If this is a mechanism applicable to other Nectin-4 ADCs, it could inform a differentiated combination approach.
- **Autophagy + Nectin-4 in gastric cancer:** New paper (BBA Mol Basis Dis, Feb 2026) shows autophagy inhibition enhances Nectin-4-MMAE efficacy in gastric cancer — early-stage mechanism with no clinical program.
- **Esophageal SCC:** 9MW2821 showed only 14% ORR — low efficacy. The Trop2 + Nectin-4 + PD-L1 co-expression data suggests combination approaches may be required; white space for a differentiated program here.

### Watch List Items

- [ ] **Corbus CRB-701 monotherapy durability data — mid-2026 (ASCO 2026).** Most important near-term readout for the field. ORR data already strong; durability and patient stratification will determine whether this heads to accelerated approval.
- [ ] **Corbus CRB-701 + Keytruda in 1L HNSCC — Q4 2026.** Combination data will set the competitive bar for any program seeking combination with PD-1 in HNSCC.
- [ ] **Corbus HNSCC registrational study initiation — mid-2026.** If initiated, CRB-701 becomes the first next-gen Nectin-4 ADC in a pivotal trial in the West.
- [ ] **Aktis NECTINIUM-2 data — Q1 2027.** First clinical data for radiopharmaceutical Nectin-4 targeting. Could validate entirely new modality.
- [ ] **9MW2821 pivotal trial readouts** (NCT06196736, NCT06592326, NCT06692166) — urothelial, cervical, TNBC. China-first, but potential global regulatory submissions.
- [ ] **ADRX-0706 Phase 1b data** — expected but not yet confirmed as presented at ASCO 2025 (dose escalation only). Look for dose expansion and efficacy data at upcoming conferences.
- [ ] **EV perioperative MIBC cisplatin-eligible FDA decision** — pending priority review.
- [ ] **IPH4502 (Innate Pharma) Phase 1 initial data** — expected 2026; Topo-I payload differentiation.
- [ ] **AVZO-103 Phase 1 dose escalation safety data** — first readout likely 2026-2027.
- [ ] **Lilly Nectin-4 ADC** — still no ClinicalTrials registration. If/when filed, signals Lilly internal development vs partnership interest.
