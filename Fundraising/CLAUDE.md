- `pitch-materials/` — decks, one-pagers, data-room prep

- `rounds/` — round-specific planning
- `references/` — supporting material, incl. `target-deep-dives/`

## Diligence answer standard

Every diligence answer leads with a single summary sentence that fully captures
the answer, then expands into bullets. Full rubric in
`style/sego-writing-style/`. Key checks: first sentence stands alone; numbers in
the first paragraph; preclinical vs clinical and imaging vs therapeutic cleanly
separated; uncertainty stated once with a control mechanism; comparisons used
where they exist (RLT vs ADC/EBRT, peptide vs mAb).
